Why teams choose the Overlook

The whole estate, for your team only

Total exclusivity

The entire fifteen-acre estate is booked for your group alone. For the length of your booking, every building and every acre belongs to you.

Everyone stays where you meet

Your team works, eats and stays in the same place. The conversation carries from the pavilion to dinner to the fire pit, and nobody has to drive anywhere.

A place people want to travel to

Flathead Lake is out the window, and Whitefish and Glacier National Park are close enough to extend the trip.

Built as a venue

The meeting space, the power, the internet and the grounds are permanent, so nothing has to be brought in for your week.

The space

What your group has to work with

The Pavilion

3,200 sq ft with panoramic lake views and dimmable lighting, adaptable from a full-group general session to a seated dinner. The tent runs clear-top or fully enclosed white-top with sides, so spring and fall work too.

The Main House — The Swan

Three levels with a gourmet kitchen, a full bar, a game room and lake-view balconies. It works well for breakout groups and evenings.

The Grounds

Heated pool and cabana, hot tub, barrel sauna, fitness room, fire pit, putting green, lawn games and walking trails for the hours between sessions.

Connectivity

Starlink is installed in every house and at the venue, so people can stay on calls and keep working.

Logistics

  • 200 AMP electrical service
  • On-site water
  • Parking for 75 cars
  • Vendor and load-in access within 50 feet of the venue
