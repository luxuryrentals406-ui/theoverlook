The package

Starting at$20,000

Exclusive use of the estate, with the venue already built. The final figure depends on your dates and the shape of the weekend, so we quote it against both.

  • The whole estate for your booking — one wedding, no other events
  • The 3,200 sq ft pavilion and the 40 × 80 ft tent, clear-top or white-top with full sides
  • Separate ceremony, cocktail and reception areas
  • The built-in bar, the estate’s tables and chairs, and four head tables
  • 200 AMP power and water on site; vendors load in within 50 feet
  • Parking for 75 cars on the property
  • Your onsite venue coordinator
  • Up to 200 guests, with an 11:00 p.m. end
  • The five houses on the property, booked separately through Flathead Lake Luxury Lodging
The day

How the day moves

From the first chair on the lawn to breakfast the next morning, all of it on one hillside.

Afternoon

Vows on the lawn above the lake, with the trees on either side and the sun behind your guests.

Early evening

Drinks out on the grass while the tent is made ready; nobody is herded into a hallway.

Dinner

The tables laid under the tent, the lake through the walls, and the light going gold.

After dark

Candles, speeches and the dance floor, until the end time your contract sets.

The morning after

The people staying on the property wake up a short walk from where the night ended.

Where everyone stays

Five houses, a short walk from the tent

Your wedding party can sleep on the property. The houses are booked through our lodging company, Flathead Lake Luxury Lodging: The Compound — the Swan, Glacier and Lakeside houses, sleeping 22 — or The Full Estate, all five, sleeping 28.
