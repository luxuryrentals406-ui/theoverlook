The package

Starting at$135,000

The whole of The Overlook for the celebration and a lakefront home for the people closest to you, under one booking.

  • Five nights across two estates, for one group
  • The Overlook — the pavilion, the tent and the grounds, yours alone for the wedding
  • The Driftwood — a 14,000 sq ft lakefront home at Woods Bay with 7 bedrooms, 9 baths, a private cove and boat slips
  • Room for 54 to stay: 28 across The Overlook’s five houses, 26 at The Driftwood
  • Helicopter transfer between the two, through WestSlope
  • Planning support from the first call to the send-off
The Driftwood

Down on the water at Woods Bay

Where the wedding party wakes up: the great room opens to the lake, the dock runs out to the boat slips, and the deck looks straight down the water.

Between the two

Twenty minutes by road, a few by air

The estates are about twenty minutes apart around the lake. WestSlope can fly your group between them, so the rehearsal dinner can be at one and the last night at the other.
