The Area

Things to do around Flathead Lake

The estate is private and self-contained, but nobody spends a whole weekend on one hillside. Here is what is actually within reach, with honest drive times.

Flathead Lake

The largest freshwater lake west of the Mississippi sits below the property. Boating and swimming are minutes away, and the water is what the pavilion looks out over — the reason the ceremony lawn faces the direction it does.

Glacier National Park

Roughly a one-hour drive to West Glacier, the west entrance — 49 miles. Close enough that guests add a day either side of the weekend, far enough that it is a day trip rather than an afternoon. Going-to-the-Sun Road is seasonal; check the park before promising anyone a drive over the pass.

Whitefish Mountain Resort

About 45 minutes from the estate. Skiing in winter, lift-served hiking and biking in summer, and the town below it is where a good share of guests end up staying when the five houses are full.

Arriving by air

Glacier Park International Airport (FCA) is 35 minutes away. For arrivals with more ceremony to them, helicopter landings and private flights over the lake are arranged through WestSlope Helicopters — the same operator that runs the transfer in the two-estate weekend package.

Without leaving the property

Most of a weekend happens here, which is the point of booking the whole estate:

  • A heated pool and a hot tub
  • A barrel sauna
  • A fitness room and a games room
  • A fire pit
  • A putting green
  • Walking trails across the fifteen acres
