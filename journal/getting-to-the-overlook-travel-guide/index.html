Getting to The Overlook: a travel guide for wedding guests

Airport, cars, shuttles and places to stay, in a form you can share with your guests.

September 2, 2026 · The Overlook at Flathead Lake

Getting to The Overlook is straightforward. Here is a guide you can share with your guests.

Fly into Glacier Park International (FCA)

The closest airport is Glacier Park International Airport in Kalispell, Montana — airport code FCA. From FCA, the estate is roughly a 35-minute drive. Put that on your wedding website.

FCA is a small airport with rental car counters in the terminal. Anyone flying in on the wedding day itself should leave extra time, since a missed connection is harder to make up at a small airport.

Renting a car vs. arranging shuttles

Both work. Which is right depends on your guest list.

Rental cars make sense when guests are arriving on different days, want to explore the valley on their own schedule, or are extending the trip. Rental cars at FCA run short in summer, so tell guests to book early.

Group shuttles make sense when a large block of guests arrives in a similar window, when you’d rather not manage a parking lot full of cars, or when there is a bar. A shuttle for the wedding evening means nobody has to drive after the reception. Some couples arrange rental cars for a few key people and a shuttle for everyone else.

  • Book shuttle service well ahead; regional operators fill up in summer.
  • Give the driver one point of contact from your side, not five.
  • Plan the last shuttle for after your music curfew, not at it.
  • Let guests know in advance that there is parking on site for 75 cars.

Where guests stay

The estate sleeps 28 guests onsite across the main house, the cabin, the tiny homes and the treehouses, usually kept for family and the wedding party. Everyone else stays nearby in Lakeside, Kalispell, Bigfork or Whitefish, and we can suggest places to stay.

Extending the trip

If guests want to stay longer, Flathead Lake has boating, swimming, lakeside restaurants and cherry stands in late summer, and the valley has plenty of hiking and small towns to visit.

Glacier National Park is another good reason to stay. West Glacier, the park’s west entrance, is roughly a one-hour drive from the estate — about 49 miles. It works as a day trip, but plan a full day rather than fitting it around wedding events, and check the park’s website first, since entry rules and conditions change from year to year.

A simple note to send your guests

You can copy this: “Fly into Glacier Park International Airport (FCA) in Kalispell, Montana — the venue is about 35 minutes away. Reserve a rental car early if you’d like to explore, and watch for shuttle details for the wedding evening. If you can, stay an extra day or two: Flathead Lake is right here, and West Glacier — the west entrance to Glacier National Park — is about an hour away, roughly 49 miles.”
