Your wedding weekend

The property empties out for you

For the length of your booking the estate is closed to everyone but your people — one wedding on the property, and no one crossing the lawn who was not invited.

That changes the shape of the day. Your ceremony happens on the lawn above the water. Cocktail hour moves to the grounds. Dinner and dancing take the pavilion and tent. Guests travel through the evening instead of sitting in one room watching it get flipped around them.

The end time is whatever your contract says it is, agreed with you when you book rather than handed to you on the day.

How the evening moves

Five acres of it, in order

The evening moves through the property rather than staying in one room. Select a marker to see where each part of it happens.

Arrival

They come down the drive

Guests park on the property — 75 cars, no shuttle contract, no field. The approach is the first thing they see, and it is already the lake.

Ceremony

The lawn faces west

Chairs set on the grass above the water, with the tree line on both sides. Late afternoon puts the sun behind the officiant, not in your guests’ eyes.

The walk down

Stone steps, not a shuttle

Ceremony and reception are a short walk apart on the same hillside. Nobody gets in a car, and the gap between the two is about the length of a drink.

Cocktails

The grounds hold the hour

The built-in bar runs while the tent is turned over. Guests spread onto the gravel and lawn instead of queuing in a corridor.

Reception

Dinner under the tent

Forty by eighty feet, clear-top or white-top with full sides. Round tables, four head tables and chairs, set before you arrive.

The Driftwood,
down on the water

The Overlook sleeps 28 up on the hill. When the guest list runs past that, The Driftwood can be added to your booking — a 14,000 sq ft lakefront home at Woods Bay that sleeps 26, with its own cove and private boat slips.

It is about twenty minutes around the lake from the venue, or a few minutes by air — we can move your group between the two by helicopter with WestSlope. Either way both estates sit under one booking, so your family and your closest people stay together instead of scattering across hotels in Whitefish and Kalispell.
